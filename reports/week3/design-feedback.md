# Sprint 1 Design Feedback Evidence

This document summarizes design-related feedback received during the Sprint 1 Customer Review with Diana.

## Approved Direction

The customer positively evaluated the MVP v1 visual direction and explicitly approved continuing with:

- light background instead of a dark interface;
- warm and pleasant color palette;
- gradient direction;
- background style;
- general movement away from a dry standard questionnaire.

The customer said that the palette, gradient, and background were surprisingly strong and that the team can continue developing this direction with small additions.

## Requested Improvements

| Area | Customer feedback | Planned response |
|---|---|---|
| Landing composition | Top visual group sticks too closely to the top | Move grouped elements lower and improve spacing |
| Logo | Current logo variants may be overloaded | Simplify the logo and avoid excessive details |
| Contrast | Text/background contrast should be checked | Verify contrast in Figma and adjust text colors if needed |
| Visual identity | Product should not look like a generic AI template | Add a stronger visual/conceptual hook or anchor |
| Similarity with another team | Some colors/elements overlap | Communicate with the other team and keep KOYASH direction distinct |
| MVP screen | One unnecessary element should be removed | Create a design/implementation task to remove it |

## Backlog Impact

The feedback should be converted into design and implementation issues. The most urgent items are:

1. Adjust landing page composition.
2. Simplify logo.
3. Check contrast and readability.
4. Preserve the approved palette and gradient.
5. Add a memorable hook/anchor.
