# Contrast and Readability Check

Project: **KOYASH**  
Team: **Sunwise (#12)**  
Sprint: **Sprint 1 / MVP v1**

This document records a design quality check based on customer feedback from the Sprint 1 Customer Review.

## Reason for the Check

During the customer review, Diana pointed out that text/background contrast is important for readability. She mentioned that some combinations, such as dark text on dark orange, may be difficult to read. The team should check contrast in Figma and adjust colors where needed.

## Areas to Check

The following MVP v1 and prototype areas should be checked:

- landing page hero text;
- main CTA button text;
- questionnaire question text;
- “why we ask” explanation text;
- answer option cards;
- budget selection cards;
- result page headings;
- product card explanation blocks;
- feedback buttons;
- any text placed on gradient or colored background.

## Planned Readability Actions

| Area | Risk | Planned action |
|---|---|---|
| Hero section | Text may compete with gradient/background | Check contrast and adjust text color if needed |
| CTA buttons | Button text must remain readable | Verify button background/text combination |
| Questionnaire cards | Small text may be hard to read | Increase contrast or simplify background |
| Product cards | Explanation text is the key content | Keep explanation block visually clear and readable |
| Feedback buttons | Secondary buttons may be too pale | Check hover/default states and text contrast |

## Acceptance Target

For MVP v1, the team will use Figma contrast tools or manual visual inspection to make sure that:

- important text is readable at normal screen brightness;
- text does not blend into colored backgrounds;
- explanation blocks remain clear;
- the interface keeps the approved warm visual direction without sacrificing readability.

## Backlog Impact

This check supports the following user stories:

- US-01 Landing page trust;
- US-02 Step-by-step questionnaire;
- US-07 Product explanations;
- US-14 Why-we-ask explanations;
- US-15 Explanation-first cards.
