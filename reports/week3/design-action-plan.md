# Design Action Plan After Customer Review

Project: **KOYASH**  
Team: **Sunwise (#12)**  
Sprint: **Sprint 1 / MVP v1**

This document converts customer feedback from the Sprint 1 Customer Review into concrete design actions.

## Customer-Approved Direction

During the review, the customer positively evaluated the current MVP v1 visual direction. The following elements should be preserved and developed further:

- light background instead of a dark interface;
- warm and pleasant color palette;
- gradient direction;
- soft background style;
- friendly and non-dry questionnaire feeling;
- overall movement toward a warm, trustworthy skincare assistant.

The customer said that the palette, gradient, and background were strong and that the team can continue working in this direction with small additions.

## Design Changes Requested by the Customer

| Area | Customer feedback | Planned response | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing composition | The top visual group sticks too closely to the top of the screen | Move grouped elements lower and improve spacing | High |
| Logo | Current logo variants may feel overloaded | Simplify the logo and remove unnecessary details | High |
| Contrast | Text/background contrast should be checked | Verify contrast in Figma and adjust colors where needed | High |
| Visual identity | The product should not look like a generic AI template | Add a stronger visual or conceptual hook | Medium |
| Similarity with another team | Some visual elements, especially colors, may overlap | Communicate with the other team and keep KOYASH visually distinct | Medium |
| MVP screen | One unnecessary element should be removed from the MVP screen | Remove or redesign the element before final MVP v1 evidence | Medium |

## Immediate Design Tasks

1. Move the main landing visual group lower.
2. Simplify the logo.
3. Check contrast and readability in Figma.
4. Preserve the approved palette, gradient, and light background.
5. Add a visual/conceptual anchor that makes KOYASH more memorable.
6. Coordinate with the other team if visual overlap remains.

## Backlog Impact

The feedback does not change the core MVP v1 scope. It creates design-polish tasks connected mainly to:

- US-01 Landing page trust;
- US-02 Step-by-step questionnaire;
- US-14 Why-we-ask explanations;
- US-15 Explanation-first cards.

The design changes should be considered part of MVP v1 polish and Sprint 2 refinement.
