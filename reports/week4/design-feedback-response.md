# Design Feedback Response

Project: KOYASH  
Team: Sunwise  
Assignment: 4  
Increment: Final MVP v1

## Context

During the customer review / facilitated UAT walkthrough, the customer reviewed the updated visual direction of KOYASH and provided feedback on the landing page, typography, logo, visual assets, and information structure.

This document summarizes how the team interpreted the design feedback and how it affects the current MVP v1 and future MVP v2 candidate work.

## Positive feedback

The customer positively evaluated the warm color direction:

- pink and light-yellow palette works well;
- the overall soft visual direction is appropriate for KOYASH;
- the palette should be preserved in future iterations.

Evidence:

- 00:13:04 — the customer said that the color palette works well and that the pink/light-yellow combination is successful.

## Improvement requests

The customer requested improvements in the following areas:

| Area | Evidence | Feedback |
|---|---:|---|
| Logo | 00:02:33 | Logo should be adjusted / made lighter |
| Hero typography | 00:03:38 | Main phrase should be visually stronger / bolder |
| Layout spacing | 00:05:04 | Text is too close to the top |
| Visual asset | 00:09:19 | Grass / visual asset should be changed |
| Information structure | 00:10:43, 00:26:52 | Landing content may need clearer separation |
| Design rules | 00:23:13 | Tone and palette rules should be considered |

## Response in MVP v1

For the final MVP v1 increment, the team keeps the warm visual direction and custom KOYASH visual assets as the main design basis.

The MVP v1 design response focuses on:

- preserving the approved warm pink/yellow palette;
- keeping the landing page friendly and non-corporate;
- presenting KOYASH as a guided skincare assistant;
- supporting the questionnaire and cosmetic bag result flow;
- documenting customer feedback for the next design iteration.

## Follow-up work

The following design improvements are treated as MVP v2 candidates:

- improve logo visibility and placement;
- increase visual hierarchy of the main headline;
- adjust hero spacing and composition;
- revise visual assets that do not match the desired tone;
- split or restructure landing information blocks;
- continue palette and typography exploration;
- validate the updated design with the customer again.

## Design decision

The team will not radically change the approved warm visual direction for MVP v1. Instead, the customer feedback is used to refine composition, typography, and information structure in the next iteration.
