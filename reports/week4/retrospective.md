Project: KOYASH

## Sprint goal

Improve and validate the KOYASH MVP v1 increment after customer feedback, deploy it for review, document quality requirements and testing evidence, conduct customer UAT / Sprint Review, and prepare the v0.2.0 release.

## What went well

- The team prepared the latest adaptive MVP v1 increment.
- The MVP was deployed to the university VM.
- Customer UAT / Sprint Review was conducted with Diana.
- The team prepared a public demo video.
- The presentation deck was prepared.
- Quality requirements and QRTs were documented.
- Week 4 documentation was consolidated.

## What did not go well

- Several MVP versions existed, which made it harder to confirm the final version.
- Documentation and release evidence were prepared very close to the deadline.
- Some evidence links had to be collected late in the process.
- The team had limited time to polish automated testing beyond backend/API checks.

## What we will improve

- Keep only one clearly marked latest MVP artifact.
- Update documentation continuously during the sprint instead of at the end.
- Create release notes earlier.
- Collect evidence links immediately after each activity.
- Automate deployment and testing more consistently.
- Add stronger end-to-end tests for the user-facing flow.
