# Customer Review Summary

Project: KOYASH  
Team: Sunwise  
Assignment: 4  
Customer: Diana  
Release: v0.2.0

## Review context

The team conducted a customer review / UAT / Sprint Review session with Diana.

The discussion focused on the current MVP v1 direction, especially the user-facing prototype, design, and experience. The MVP shown during the review was based on the latest adapted KOYASH visual direction.

## Main feedback themes

### 1. Design direction

The customer feedback confirmed that the project should continue moving toward a warm, soft, friendly visual style.

The design should avoid looking like a generic AI-generated template and should better communicate the KOYASH identity.

### 2. Landing page communication

The landing page should clearly explain what KOYASH does and why the user should start the questionnaire.

The main CTA and first impression remain important for future improvements.

### 3. Visual clarity

The interface should remain readable, clear, and visually balanced.

Future improvements should continue checking contrast, layout composition, and the hierarchy of text and visual elements.

### 4. Questionnaire flow

The questionnaire should feel like a guided story rather than a dry form.

The user should understand the purpose of the questions and feel that the product is helping them build a personal cosmetic bag.

### 5. MVP v1 direction

The customer review validated the general direction of MVP v1 and provided a development vector for the next iterations.

## Decisions

- Keep the latest adaptive visual direction.
- Use the deployed MVP v1 as the Sprint 2 increment.
- Continue improving design clarity and landing page communication.
- Keep product explanations as an important part of recommendation trust.
- Use customer UAT feedback to update the future roadmap.

## Outcome

The customer UAT / Sprint Review confirmed that KOYASH MVP v1 is ready to be submitted as the Assignment 4 increment, with documented next steps for future improvement.
