# LLM Usage Report — Week 3

## Summary

AI/LLM tools were used during Assignment 3 to support documentation, planning, transcript cleanup, and content structuring tasks. AI was used as an assistant, not as an autonomous author or decision maker.

## Usage Details

### 1. Assignment Analysis and Role Clarification

- **Tool:** Notion AI
- **Task:** Analysed Assignment 3 requirements and identified responsibilities for data collection, backend, design, documentation, GitLab workflow, customer review, release, and reporting.
- **Output:** Structured breakdown of assignment requirements and per-role task lists.

### 2. Documentation Drafting

- **Tool:** Notion AI
- **Tasks:**
  - Generated `docs/user-stories.md` index from backlog materials.
  - Generated `docs/definition-of-done.md` based on Scrum and course requirements.
  - Generated `docs/roadmap.md` from Sprint backlog and MVP version structure.
  - Generated `CHANGELOG.md` skeleton and Week 3 update entries.
  - Generated issue templates and an extended MR template.
  - Generated `reports/week3/reflection.md` and `reports/week3/retrospective.md` drafts.
- **Output:** Draft documents reviewed and adjusted by the team before committing.

### 3. Sprint Review Preparation

- **Tool:** Notion AI
- **Task:** Generated Sprint Review interview questions for the customer meeting.
- **Output:** Question list covering MVP v1 feedback, design validation, scope confirmation, and Sprint 2 planning. The team used the list as preparation but conducted the meeting naturally.

### 4. Transcript Cleanup and Translation

- **Tool:** Notion AI
- **Task:** Helped clean the imperfect Russian transcript, correct speaker roles, remove irrelevant fragments, and translate the sanitized version into English.
- **Output:** `reports/week3/customer-review-transcript.md` draft and `reports/week3/customer-review-summary.md` draft.

### 5. Workflow Planning

- **Tool:** Notion AI
- **Task:** Helped split Assignment 3 work between five team members and design a minimal GitLab MR/review cycle so that every member has a commit, issue-linked MR, review, and meaningful comment.
- **Output:** Work distribution plan and ready-to-copy issue/MR descriptions.

## Team Policy

All AI-generated content was reviewed by a team member before being committed to the repository. AI was not used to write or generate production code. Product decisions, customer communication, implementation choices, final wording, and submission responsibility remain with the team.
