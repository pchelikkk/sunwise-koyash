## Customer validation interpretation

The session is treated as a facilitated customer validation walkthrough. The customer reviewed the demonstrated user-facing flow, gave feedback, and clarified expectations.

The strongest accepted point is the warm color direction. The main follow-up work concerns landing composition, visual hierarchy, information structure, visual assets, and filtering.

## Roadmap impact

The following items are treated as MVP v2 candidates:

- deeper recommendation filtering;
- improved visual hierarchy;
- improved logo treatment;
- revised landing page information architecture;
- additional design exploration based on customer comments;
- fuller frontend/backend integration;
- browser-based end-to-end tests.

## Summary

The customer review produced actionable feedback for both the current MVP increment and future roadmap items. Design-related feedback was linked to the final MVP direction, while filtering was documented as a high-priority product capability for future implementation.
