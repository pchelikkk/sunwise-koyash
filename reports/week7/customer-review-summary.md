# Week 7 customer review summary

Date: 17 July 2026
Evidence: 30:45 meeting recording and timestamped transcript

The customer reviewed the complete user journey, replacement behavior, product
sources, account history and the protected analytics dashboard. She explicitly
accepted the current product with small refinements and considered the team ready
for Demo Day and submission.

The most important product decision was to sharpen KOYASH's boundary. The public
catalog and favorites do not belong in the primary experience because the brand
helps a person receive a ready routine; it is not a cosmetics manufacturer or
shop. Product details and store links remain available from the recommended
routine.

The customer requested four concrete follow-ups: reveal the result of guided skin
diagnosis, deliver post-filtering JSON for prompt review, preserve a guest routine
after sign-in/registration, and support one-action dataset import from the admin
area. All four are implemented and covered by automated tests.

The customer highlighted the admin dashboard as a strong differentiator and asked
the team to show it during the final demo. The dashboard therefore remains a
first-class protected product surface rather than a secondary technical screen.

Repository migration to GitHub is recorded as a separate handover action because
it requires a destination account, repository name and access decision. The
university VM deployment was explicitly accepted as sufficient for Demo Day.
