# Reflection

Project: KOYASH  
Team: Sunwise  
Assignment: 4  
Release: v0.2.0

## What we achieved

During Assignment 4, the team prepared the Sprint 2 increment of KOYASH for final review.

The main achievements were:

- prepared the latest adaptive MVP v1 prototype;
- deployed the MVP to the university VM;
- conducted customer UAT / Sprint Review with Diana;
- prepared quality requirements;
- prepared quality requirement tests;
- documented testing and UAT scenarios;
- prepared public demo and presentation materials;
- prepared release notes for v0.2.0.

## What went well

The team was able to quickly consolidate the current MVP state and connect it to the Assignment 4 requirements.

The latest prototype reflects the customer-facing direction better than the earlier generic version. It presents KOYASH as a warmer and more guided skincare recommendation experience.

The customer review helped validate the product direction and clarified what should be improved next.

## What was difficult

The biggest challenge was combining product work, documentation, deployment, testing evidence, UAT, release preparation, and presentation materials in a limited amount of time.

Another challenge was aligning repository artifacts with the actual latest MVP version, because several prototype versions existed during the project.

## What we learned

The team learned that release preparation is not only about code. It also requires:

- clear documentation;
- traceability between issues, tests, release, and UAT;
- deployment evidence;
- customer validation;
- realistic scope control.

## Future improvements

Future iterations should focus on:

- improving the landing page communication;
- continuing visual polish;
- strengthening product recommendation logic;
- adding real product data;
- adding full end-to-end tests;
- improving deployment automation;
- preparing a more production-like backend/frontend integration.
