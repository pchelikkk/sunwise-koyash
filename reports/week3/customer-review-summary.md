# Customer Review Summary — Sprint 1 / MVP v1

Project: **KOYASH**  
Team: **Sunwise (#12)**  
Customer: **Diana Minnakhmetova**  
Review type: **Sprint 1 Customer Review**  
Language: English summary based on a Russian live meeting  
Recording consent: The team asked for permission to record the meeting, and the customer granted permission.

## Goal of the Review

The goal of the Sprint 1 Customer Review was to validate the MVP v1 direction and collect feedback before finalizing Week 3 deliverables. The meeting focused on two areas:

1. **MVP v1 functionality** — the current working flow: landing/start page, questionnaire, answer confirmation, recommendation result, product-card interactions, and feedback buttons.
2. **Design direction** — the updated visual style, color palette, light background, gradient, logo direction, composition, and future design improvements.

## What Was Demonstrated

The team demonstrated and discussed:

- the current MVP v1 flow prepared for the assignment;
- the landing/start page direction;
- questionnaire logic and step-by-step flow;
- answer confirmation logic;
- result-page direction and product-card interaction buttons;
- early design variants, logo options, palette, gradient, and background;
- Figma/design comments and planned visual improvements.

## Main Customer Feedback

### Positive Feedback

- The customer liked the overall visual direction.
- The customer specifically approved the **light background**, **color palette**, **gradient**, and **background style**.
- The customer was positively surprised by MVP v1 colors and said this direction can be developed further.
- The questionnaire flow and MVP logic were considered understandable.
- The customer confirmed that the team can continue using the selected palette with small additions.

### Requested Improvements

- Move the group of top visual elements lower because they currently stick too closely to the top of the screen.
- Simplify the logo and avoid overloading it.
- Rework the initial page in the same approved visual style.
- Check text/background contrast in Figma and keep readability high.
- Avoid a generic “AI design” look and show the team’s own expertise.
- Add a stronger visual or conceptual **hook / anchor** that makes KOYASH more memorable.
- Remove one unnecessary element from the MVP screen shown during the review.
- Talk to another team because some visual elements, especially colors, are partially similar.

## User Stories Feedback

The customer reviewed the User Stories and said they were strong overall. She left multiple comments that the team should process. The comments are useful for refinement and should be reflected in Sprint 2 planning and follow-up backlog updates.

## Backlog Impact

The review does not require restarting the project or changing the core MVP v1 scope. Instead, it confirms the current direction and creates several follow-up tasks:

- keep MVP v1 rule-based and questionnaire-driven;
- continue the approved palette, gradient, and light background;
- add design polish tasks to the backlog;
- update landing composition and logo simplicity;
- add contrast/readability check as a design quality task;
- consider US-08, US-09, and US-10 during Sprint 2 planning after finishing MVP v1 release.
